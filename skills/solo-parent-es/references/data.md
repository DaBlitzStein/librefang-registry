| Family Type | Total Weeks |
|-------------|-------------|
| Two-parent (per parent) | 19 weeks |
| Single-parent (sole parent) | 32 weeks |

Breakdown: 6 mandatory + 22 flexible + 4 additional (until child turns 8).

Retroactive: August 2, 2024.

## Demographics

- 18-25% of households with children are single-parent
- >80% headed by women (monomarentales)
- 30% lower income vs two-parent households
- 52.7% at risk of poverty or exclusion (AROPE rate, INE ECV 2024)
- 43% in Save the Children programs live in extreme poverty (<€400/month)
