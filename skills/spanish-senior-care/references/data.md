## System Statistics (2025)

- 1,784,369 people with recognized dependency
- 1,635,462 receiving effective services
- 258,167 on waiting lists (152,693 main SAAD list)
- 32,704 died waiting in 2025 (one every 16 minutes)
- Average processing time: 341 days (legal limit: 180)
- 254,000+ have died waiting in the last 5 years
- State contribution: 26.96% (legal obligation: 50%)
- Total spending: €12,902M (0.81% GDP)
